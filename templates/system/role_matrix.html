Role Permission Matrix

What each system role can do in FANSC. Use this when assigning a role to a new user.

Staff & User Accounts
President Operational head
Approves claims, overrides decisions, signs off on payouts. Highest operational authority.
Admin Administrative
Manages user accounts, beneficiaries, registrations, and reports.
Tech. Admin Technical Administrator
Full system access including network/HTTPS status, database health, and configuration.
Staff Frontline
Registers beneficiaries and runs verifications. No admin or reporting access.
