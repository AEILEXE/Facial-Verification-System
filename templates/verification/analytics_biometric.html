{# ── L. Methodology & Limitations ────────────────────────────────────── #}
Methodology & Limitations
  • This dataset is a controlled labeled evaluation, not live operational traffic.
  • Results are specific to this evaluation session — they are not a claim about nationwide/general-population performance.
  • Manual Review is a deliberate third outcome, not folded into either success or failure.
  • Matcher performance (raw FaceNet decision) and final FANS-C performance (after post-score safety rules) are shown separately — see "Matcher → Final FANS-C Decision Transitions" above.
  • ROC/AUC/EER characterize the matcher's continuous score only, not the deployed three-zone operating policy.
  • {{ report.presentation_attack_gate_metrics.active_challenge_attestation_limitation }}
  • No raw face media (photo/video/frame) is stored by this controlled-evaluation workflow.
  • Claim Recording Reliability is not retrospectively computed from historical records — see the note above.
  • Real production Accuracy/FAR/FRR/PAD performance requires completed human-participant controlled testing; see the evaluation session status banner above for this evaluation session's current collection state.
